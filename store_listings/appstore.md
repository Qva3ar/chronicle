## Description

**Your brain is full. Chrono takes the load off.**

You don't need another to-do list. You need a system — one place where your notes, daily routines, and long-term goals work together instead of living in separate apps you forget to open.

Chrono is a productivity app built by someone who kept forgetting things, losing notes, and ignoring reminders. It's designed around one idea: if the tool isn't fast and frictionless, you won't use it.

**NOTES THAT ACTUALLY WORK**
Capture thoughts instantly. Tag them with one tap. Find them later without scrolling through chaos. No folders, no nested hierarchies — just fast, clean note-taking that stays out of your way.

**ROUTINES THAT STICK**
Morning exercises. Supplements. Language practice. Set it once, and Chrono reminds you — not just once, but repeatedly until you actually do it. Missed the 9 AM reminder? Get another at 9:30. And 10:00. Your routine doesn't care about your excuses, and neither does Chrono.

**GOALS WITH SESSIONS**
Reading. Studying. Deep work. These aren't tasks you check off — they're activities you build over time. Set a session length, get a notification when it ends, and decide: one more round, or done for today? No more forgotten timers running for 14 hours.

**AI ASSISTANT — YOUR NOTES AS CONTEXT**
Connect your own API key and let AI work with your actual notes. Summarize, translate, rewrite, or brainstorm — with your own data as context. Your notes become a knowledge base, not just a pile of text.

**100% OFFLINE. 100% PRIVATE.**
Everything is stored on your device. No cloud sync, no account required, no data harvesting. Your thoughts stay yours.

**WHY CHRONO?**
- Not a planner. Not a to-do app. A productivity system.
- Built for speed: open → write → done.
- Smart recurring reminders that won't let you slip.
- Session-based goal tracking for long-term activities.
- AI that actually knows your context.
- Offline-first. No sign-up. No cloud.

Stop planning to be productive. Start being productive.
