## Full Description

Your brain is full. Chrono takes the load off.

You don't need another to-do list. You need a system — one place where notes, daily routines, and long-term goals work together instead of living in five separate apps you forget to open.

▸ NOTES — capture fast, tag instantly, find everything
Write it down before you forget. Add tags with one tap. No folders, no complicated structure — just clean, fast notes that stay organized without effort.

▸ ROUTINES — reminders that don't give up on you
Swipe away a reminder and forget about it? Not anymore. Chrono sends follow-up reminders at intervals you set. Morning workout at 9 AM, reminder again at 9:30, and again at 10:00. It's persistent — because your habits should be too.

▸ GOALS — track what takes more than a day
Reading, studying, learning a language — these aren't checkboxes. They're activities built session by session. Start a timed session, get notified when it ends, and choose: continue or stop. No more timers running all night because you forgot to hit stop.

▸ AI ASSISTANT — powered by your own notes
Bring your own API key. Ask AI to summarize, translate, or rewrite — using your notes as context. Your second brain gets a brain of its own.

▸ FULLY OFFLINE & PRIVATE
No account. No cloud. No tracking. Everything lives on your device. Your data is yours — period.

★ Why people switch to Chrono:
✓ One app instead of five
✓ Notes + routines + goals in a single workflow
✓ Recurring reminders that actually work
✓ Session-based timers for deep work
✓ AI integration with your own context
✓ Offline-first — works without internet
✓ Clean UI, zero bloat

Built by someone who tried Google Keep, Notion, and Obsidian — and needed something faster, simpler, and more persistent.

Chrono. Your digital exoskeleton.
